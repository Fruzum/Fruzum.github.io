Lead a 2 hour session for women in year 13 of school. To show them the wonders of game development and show of programming as a skill women can do.

## What the workshop covered

- Unreal Engine basics
- An introduction to Blueprints for students with no coding background
- In this session we created a game where the players can shoot a target to increase their score
