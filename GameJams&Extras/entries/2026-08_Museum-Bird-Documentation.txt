TITLE: Museum Bird Documentation - Volunteer
DATE_LABEL: Aug 2026
BLURB: Cataloguing banded bird skins and bones in a museum collection, recording band and museum numbers to support future research.
===POPUP===
Volunteer work cataloguing the museum's bird collection - a change of
pace from game development, but the same underlying instinct: careful,
methodical documentation that makes future work easier for someone else.

## What the work involves

- Examining bird skins (taxidermy) and bird bones held in the museum's
  collection
- Checking each specimen for a leg band
- Recording the band number alongside the museum's own catalogue number

This creates a record of which birds in the collection are banded,
so future researchers know exactly what's available to study without
having to re-examine the whole collection themselves.
