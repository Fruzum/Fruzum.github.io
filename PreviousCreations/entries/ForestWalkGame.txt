===POPUP===
A game about walking through real world forests.

I have taking real world satellite data to get the height map of Wellington. Then I used map data to get the actual real world walking track data. I have combined these and am in the process of putting a path along the route so that the player can walk without being able to stray too far from the real world path.

I need to shape the landscape more. I need to place trees and bushes. I would also like to add birds and insects.

It is progressing well and I am enjoying creating it.
