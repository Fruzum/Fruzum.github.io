About

Gameplay-focused developer with industry experience in both Unity and Unreal, specialising in performant, polished, and engaging mechanics for players and fellow developers alike.

My work spans PlanetSmith (Incandescent Games) — an open-world survival game built on a hex-grid voxel planet — Axon (Exsurgo), a neurofeedback device helping chronic pain sufferers, and Wanderer (Mighty Eyes), a time-travel VR game. Across each, I built AI systems, custom editor tooling, and gameplay mechanics that hold up under real player feedback.

I hold a Bachelor of Software Engineering from Media Design School, and am comfortable moving between Unity ECS/Jobs/Burst, Unreal Blueprints and C++, and the strict design constraints of medical-grade software.
